🎨 Palette: Add title hover tooltips and accessible labels to AgentOverview persona count badge

💡 What:
Added explicit `title` hover tooltips and `aria-label` attributes to the registered/filtered persona count badge in `AgentOverview.tsx` in `apps/sak_agent_dashboard`. Updated corresponding unit tests in `components.test.tsx`.

🎯 Why:
Desktop mouse users hovering over the count pill in `AgentOverview` were missing context on whether the count represented total registered personas or a subset filtered by persona selection. The tooltip clarifies active vs total count states.

📸 Before/After:
- Before: `<div className="...">6 Personas Registered</div>` (No tooltip or ARIA description)
- After: `<div title="6 total agent personas registered" aria-label="6 total agent personas registered">6 Personas Registered</div>` (Full hover tooltip and accessible ARIA description)

♿ Accessibility:
Provides screen readers and desktop users with unambiguous context regarding total vs filtered persona counts.
