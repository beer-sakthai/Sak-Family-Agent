# Code of Conduct

This project grew out of a hard chapter in my life, and it's built on six values:
**Dream, Hope, Care, Joy, Trust, and Growth.** I'd love for everyone here to carry
a little of them.

## The short version

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good intent. A gentle word goes far.
- **Everyone is welcome** — every background, every skill level, every first-timer.
- **Care over criticism.** Suggest and encourage; don't shame.
- **No harassment, hate, cruelty, or discrimination.** None.
- Remember this is a small, personal project made with heart — treat it, and each
  other, gently.

## If something goes wrong

If someone's behaviour here is hurtful, please email me privately:
**gensandee@gmail.com**. I'll listen and do my best to make it right.

Thank you for being here, and for being kind. 💛
