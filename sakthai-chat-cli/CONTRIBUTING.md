# Contributing

Thank you for being here. 💛

This is a **personal project** — a reminder of my own journey, and something I
share in the hope it helps someone else too. **Every recommendation and every
suggestion is genuinely welcome.** You don't need permission to have an idea here.

## How you can help

- **Have an idea, a fix, or feedback?** Open an issue and just tell me. No idea is
  too small, no question is silly.
- **Want to send a change?** Open a pull request — I'll look at it when I can.
- **Just want to say hello or share your own story?** That's welcome too.

## A few gentle notes

- It's just me maintaining this, so replies may be slow. Thank you for your
  patience — it's not indifference, it's one person doing their best.
- Please be kind (see `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`). We build in the spirit of Dream,
  Hope, Care, Joy, Trust, and Growth.
- Keep things **free and simple** where you can — this project is meant to run at
  no cost, on your own machine.
- Never include secrets, tokens, or keys in issues or pull requests
  (see `SECURITY.md`).

However you show up — an idea, a fix, or just kindness — thank you. This project
is a little like my life: still being built, and better with others in it.
