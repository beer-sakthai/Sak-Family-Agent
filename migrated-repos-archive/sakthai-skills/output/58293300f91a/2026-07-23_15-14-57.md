## HF Space Deep Dive: hexgrad/Kokoro-TTS

**[hexgrad/Kokoro-TTS](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hexgrad/Kokoro-TTS)** showcases **Kokoro**, an open-weight text-to-speech model with just 82 million parameters — remarkably compact yet delivering quality competitive with much larger TTS models. Built on StyleTTS2-LJSpeech as its base, Kokoro punches far above its weight: the entire model trains in ~1000 A100-GPU hours for under $1,000 total, and inference costs less than $1 per million characters (~$0.06/hour of audio). The Space offers 30 distinct voices across American and British English (both male and female), with features like phoneme-level pronunciation customization via Markdown-like `[word](/kˈOkəɹO/)` syntax, stress-level adjustments, speed control from 0.5x–2x, and ZeroGPU acceleration that keeps it free to use.

Technically, the Space runs on **Gradio 5.24** with a clever architecture: the `kokoro` and `misaki` libraries handle the TTS pipeline (phonemization, voicepack loading, waveform generation), while the `@spaces.GPU` decorator provides 30-second ZeroGPU allocations for GPU inference with transparent CPU fallback when quota runs out. The app is split into two tabs — *Generate* (single audio output with token display for pronunciation debugging) and *Stream* (real-time streaming with cancel support). It includes preset buttons for sample texts (Gatsby, Frankenstein, random quotes), a char limit that only applies to duplicates (5000 chars), and an open API for programmatic access.

What makes this Space stand out is **cost efficiency without quality sacrifice** — Kokoro is Apache 2.0 licensed, deployable anywhere from production APIs to edge devices, and already powers commercial TTS services under $0.65–0.80/Mchars. The demo itself is a masterclass in thoughtful UX: pronunciation transparency via the "Tokenize" button lets users tweak phonemes interactively, the voice selector uses emoji flags for quick recognition, and the duplicate-friendly design encourages remixing. For anyone building voice apps on a budget, this is the current gold standard.
