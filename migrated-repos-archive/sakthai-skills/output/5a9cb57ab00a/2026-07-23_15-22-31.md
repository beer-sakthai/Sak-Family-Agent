## Self Gradient Forcing: Native Long Video Extrapolation

**Paper:** [2607.20368](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.20368) · arXiv: [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20368) — 14 authors led by Junhao Zhuang, Shiyi Zhang, and Nan Duan

**The problem.** Autoregressive video diffusion models train on their own rollouts (Self Forcing) to reduce exposure bias — but there's a fundamental blind spot. When the model generates a frame, its latents get encoded into a key-value cache that future frames read from. Under Self Forcing, that cache is treated as *frozen state*: future-frame losses have no gradient path back to supervise how earlier frames should write their latents into better memory. The authors call this the **historical context-gradient gap** — the model can't learn to encode "helpful" causal memory because the loss signal never reaches the encoding step.

**The fix — Self Gradient Forcing (SGF).** SGF is a two-pass training strategy that bridges the gap without backpropagating through the full serial rollout (which would be intractably memory-heavy). Pass 1 runs a no-gradient autoregressive rollout identical to inference, sampling a random denoising exit step and recording both the self-generated context and the noisy latents fed at that step. Pass 2 then reconstructs gradients through a truncated subgraph: it uses the generated context as stop-gradient clean-latent input, recomputes the context KV representations, and enables future-to-context causal attention with real gradients flowing backward. The result: future-frame losses now teach the model how to write better keys and values into its causal memory — exactly what was missing.

**Why it matters.** The results are striking: with only a **5-second training window**, SGF extrapolates to videos lasting *several minutes* — coherently holding subject identity, background layout, and temporal stability far beyond what Self Forcing achieves. This isn't about scaling up data or compute; it's about surfacing an architectural gradient that was silently missing. For the autoregressive video generation community, SGF closes a hole that every Self Forcing–style method has been training around, and it does so with a lightweight two-pass that doesn't require longer rollouts or larger models. If you're building autoregressive video models (or any sequential latent generative model with causal masking), this is the paper that tells you your loss has been incomplete.
