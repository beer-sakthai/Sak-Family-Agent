## HF Papers of the Day — 2026-07-23 (Evening)

The evening arXiv digest landed with a video extrapolation breakthrough, the first scaling laws for hypernetwork knowledge injection, and a full-stack DeepSeek-V4 post-training run on Ascend NPUs.

### 1. Self Gradient Forcing: Native Long Video Extrapolation
**HF:** https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.20368 · **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20368 · ⭐ 17

Autoregressive video diffusion's Achilles heel: future losses can't supervise how earlier latents encode their KV cache, creating a "context-gradient gap." SGF fixes this with a two-pass strategy — rollout without gradients, then reconstruct the context-gradient signal at a sampled exit step without backprop through the full serial chain. **Why it matters:** From a **5-second training window**, SGF extrapolates to **multi-minute** videos with dramatically better subject identity and temporal stability vs standard Self Forcing. Clean, efficient fix for the core bottleneck in long-form autoregressive video.

### 2. Scaling Laws for Hypernetwork-Based Knowledge Injection in LLMs
**HF:** https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.19604 · **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19604 · ⭐ 13

First rigorous scaling-law study for hypernetwork-based knowledge injection. Instead of fine-tuning the target model, train a hypernetwork to emit a fixed LoRA adapter from a fact corpus. MegaWikiQA dataset: tens of millions of multi-hop QA pairs across 39 domains from Wikidata5M. **Key finding:** hypernetworks obey predictive power-law scaling and **outperform LoRA/full fine-tuning on OOD generalization** with steeper scaling exponents — while keeping target weights frozen. Opens a principled alternative to destructive fine-tuning at scale.

### 3. SLAI T-Rex: Full-Parameter Post-training of DeepSeek-V4 on Ascend SuperPOD
**HF:** https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.20145 · **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145 · ⭐ 12 — 65 authors

Makes DeepSeek-V4-Flash run on Ascend NPU SuperPOD (not GPU) with 34.22% MFU (2.93× baseline improvement) via hierarchical parallelism + computation-communication orchestration. On that infra, CPT + SFT for Operations Research tasks yields a specialized model scoring **71.81% zero-shot Pass@1** — beating GPT-5.4-Mini by 3.98 pp and base DeepSeek-V4-Flash by 11.27 pp. **Why it matters:** Blueprint for training frontier-scale MoE on non-GPU infra + proof that domain-specialized OR post-training delivers outsized gains.
