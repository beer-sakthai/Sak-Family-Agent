### 2. Self Gradient Forcing: Native Long Video Extrapolation
**🔗** https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.20368 — **26 votes**

Fixes a blind spot in autoregressive video diffusion: the "historical context-gradient gap." Current Self Forcing treats cached KV as frozen rollout state — future losses can't steer how earlier latents are written into memory. SGF adds a second training pass that restores this gradient signal. Result: **5-second training window → extrapolates to several-minute videos** with strong subject identity and temporal stability.

### 3. Beyond Relevance-Centric Retrieval: Rubric-Oriented Document Set Selection and Ranking
**🔗** https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.19747 — **22 votes · 5 comments (most discussed)**

Old paradigm: score docs independently, aggregate via nDCG. This paper argues that's broken for the LLM-as-consumer era — missing inter-document interactions like redundancy and complementarity. They built **SetwiseEvalKit** (28K rubrics across 3 levels, 9 dimensions), tested 12 rerankers (best ≤45% coverage!), then introduced **Rubric4Setwise** — training-free, converts rubric criteria into selection signals, achieves SOTA with fewer docs and fewer search rounds.
