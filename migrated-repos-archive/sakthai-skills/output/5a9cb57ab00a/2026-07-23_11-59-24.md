## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 1. SLAI T-Rex: Full-Parameter Post-training of the DeepSeek-V4 Family on Ascend SuperPOD
[`2607.20145`](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.20145) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145)

**What it is:** End-to-end optimization for full-parameter post-training of trillion-parameter MoE models (DeepSeek-V4 family) on an Ascend NPU SuperPOD — not GPUs. Tackles memory pressure, non-overlapped communication, and inefficient kernel execution at scale.

**Why it matters:** Proof that Ascend NPU clusters can handle frontier-scale training. Major signal for AI infra diversification away from NVIDIA. If you work on distributed training or China AI infra, this is the paper today.

### 2. Beyond Euclidean Clipping: Overcoming Exploration Collapse in LLM RL via Riemannian Isometric Policy Optimization
[`2607.10169`](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.10169) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10169)

**What it is:** Identifies the root cause of PPO-Clip's exploration collapse in LLM RL — it measures policy discrepancy with Euclidean metrics, which are geometrically inconsistent with the policy manifold. Proposes Riemannian Isometric Policy Optimization (RIPO) to fix it.

**Why it matters:** First *principled* diagnosis of a problem that everyone's been hacking around heuristically. If you do RLHF or RL for reasoning models, read this.

---

### 3. ATSplat: Compact Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting with Adaptive Token Expansion
[`2607.20417`](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.20417) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20417)

**What it is:** A feed-forward 3DGS method that regains scene-adaptive primitive allocation via a learnable token expansion mechanism, decoupling Gaussian count from image resolution. Prior feed-forward methods pinned Gaussians to pixels — ATSplat lets them grow where needed.

**Why it matters:** Closes the gap between per-scene optimization 3DGS (adaptive densification) and fast feed-forward 3DGS. Practical for real-time novel-view synthesis.
