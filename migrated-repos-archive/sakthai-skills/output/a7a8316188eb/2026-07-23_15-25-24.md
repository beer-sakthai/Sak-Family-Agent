## Trending Deep Dive: `poolside/Laguna-S-2.1`

**Rank:** #3 on HF trending (trendingScore: 466) · **Released:** 2026-07-22

Laguna S 2.1 is Poolside's mid-size agentic coding MoE — 118B total parameters with only 8B activated per token, sitting between Laguna XS 2.1 (33B-A3B) and the upcoming Laguna M.1 (225B-A23B). Its architecture uses a token-choice router with softplus gating over **256 routed experts** (top-10 selected per token) plus one shared expert. It packs 48 transformer layers in a 1:3 global-to-sliding-window ratio (12 global + 36 SWA with window 512), grouped-query attention with 8 KV heads, and a **1,048,576-token context window**. The 100K-token Laguna family tokenizer feeds a vocabulary of 100,352. Custom `LagunaForCausalLM` architecture with `trust-remote-code` — vLLM, SGLang, TRT-LLM, and llama.cpp all support it natively, and a **DFlash speculative decoding** draft model is available for latency reduction. Quantized variants span FP8, NVFP4, INT4, and GGUF, all under the permissive **OpenMDW-1.1 license** (commercial use allowed).

On benchmarks, Laguna S 2.1 punches well above its 8B-active weight: **70.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1**, **78.5% SWE-bench Multilingual**, **59.4% SWE-Bench Pro**, **40.4% DeepSWE**, and **49.7% Toolathlon Verified** — competitive with models 3–6× larger in active parameters. It trails frontier models like Claude Fable 5 and Kimi K3 (which are 50B+ active) but significantly beats Inkling (975B-A41B) on several agentic coding metrics, making it one of the most parameter-efficient coding MoEs available today. Key differentiators include native interleaved reasoning (thinking tokens between tool calls, controllable per-request with `enable_thinking`) and the mixed SWA/global attention layout that enables long-context retrieval without paying full quadratic cost.

**Why this matters:** Laguna S 2.1 represents a sweet spot in the open-source agentic coding landscape — small enough to run on consumer multi-GPU setups (236 GB BF16 → fits on 4× H100 with tensor parallelism), yet strong enough to rival much larger models. Its OpenMDW license avoids the restrictions of many coding-model licenses, and the native tool-use + reasoning pipeline is purpose-built for software engineering agents. For anyone building coding agents on a budget, this is the model to watch.
