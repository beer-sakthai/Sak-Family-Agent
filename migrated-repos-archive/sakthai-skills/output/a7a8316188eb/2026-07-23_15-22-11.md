## Deep Dive: `baidu/Unlimited-OCR`

**Baidu Unlimited-OCR** is the #1 trending model on Hugging Face (2,834 ⭐, 2.41M downloads) — a vision-language transformer that solves one of the most stubborn problems in LLM-based document OCR: the ballooning KV cache that makes long-sequence transcription untenable. At its core is **Reference Sliding Window Attention (R-SWA)**, a novel attention mechanism that replaces every attention layer in the decoder with a constant-memory sliding window. Unlike standard transformers where the KV cache grows linearly with output length (consuming GBs for multi-page documents), R-SWA keeps KV cache *flat* at a 128-token window across the entire decoding pass. The result is a model that can transcribe dozens of pages in a single forward pass within 32K context — no chunking, no batching, no memory explosion.

Architecturally, Unlimited-OCR pairs a **dual vision encoder** (CLIP-L-14 @ 224px + SAM ViT-B @ 1024px) with a **DeepSeek V2-based MoE decoder** (12 layers, 1280 hidden, 64 routed experts + 2 shared, top-6 active per token, 10 attention heads). The projector is a lean linear MLP (2048→1280). Two inference modes are supported: **"gundam"** (640px crop-based, optimized for single-image dense OCR) and **"base"** (1024px full-resolution, for multi-page/PDF pipelines). It runs on vLLM, SGLang, and native Transformers, with ready-made Docker images for Hopper GPUs and a permissive **MIT license**.

The paper (arXiv 2606.23050) positions R-SWA as a *general-purpose parsing attention* mechanism — the decoder innovation is not OCR-specific and could equally apply to ASR, translation, or any long-horizon structured output task. By proving that a constant-memory decoder can match or exceed full-attention OCR quality while enabling single-pass multi-document transcription, Unlimited-OCR charts a practical path beyond the memory wall that has constrained end-to-end vision-language models for document understanding.
