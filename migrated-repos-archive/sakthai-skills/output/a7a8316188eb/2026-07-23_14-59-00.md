## 🔥 Deep Dive: `upstage/Solar-Open2-250B`

**Trending Score:** 339 · **Released:** 22 Jul 2026 · **Status:** < 24 hours old, 392 likes, surging fast.

### What It Is

Solar Open 2 is Upstage's new open-weight flagship — a 250B total / 15B active hybrid-attention MoE purpose-built for **agentic workloads**. It's the direct successor to Solar Open 1 (102B) and the first model to ship a production-grade **interleaved softmax + linear attention** stack natively through Transformers and vLLM. The architecture is 48 layers deep with 321 experts (320 routed + 1 shared, top-8 activation), hidden dimension 4096, and a 196K-token vocabulary — trained on ~12 trillion tokens across English, Korean, and Japanese. 2 million GPU-hours on NVIDIA B200s.

### What Makes It Special

Three innovations converge here:

1. **Hybrid-Attention MoE** — Every group of 4 layers follows a `[softmax, linear×3]` pattern. The linear-attention layers (3 out of every 4) use a recurrent state that encodes token order *intrinsically*, so positional encoding is eliminated entirely (**NoPE**). Only 12 of 48 layers need a KV cache — long-context memory cost is ~¼ of a comparably sized all-softmax model. This is what enables the **1M-token context window** without exploding memory.

2. **Selective Weight Initialization** — Upstage didn't train from scratch. They carried over only the **2.3% of weights** from Solar Open 1 (102B) that survive the architectural change; everything else was randomly initialized. This gave a dramatically better starting point, accelerating early convergence despite the 2.5× parameter increase.

3. **Agent-native reasoning** — It exposes `reasoning_effort` (none/high) through the chat template, generating `<|think|>` blocks for chain-of-thought before answering. Combined with native tool-call parsing and vLLM integration, it's built to slot into SWE-bench and MCP pipelines without wrappers. **SWE-Bench Verified: 70.4%** — competitive with DeepSeek-V4-Flash (73.8%) and ahead of Command A+ (14.4%) and Mistral Medium 3.5 (69.6%). **APEX-Agents: 16.6%** — tops the open field.

### How to Use It

Requires ≥4× H200 (141 GB ea.). The recommended path is the [Upstage vLLM fork](https://github.com/UpstageAI/vllm/tree/v0.22.0-solar-open2) with expert-parallel and Triton MoE backend:

```bash
vllm serve upstage/Solar-Open2-250B \
  --tensor-parallel-size 8 \
  --enable-expert-parallel \
  --moe-backend triton \
  --reasoning-parser solar_open2 \
  --tool-call-parser solar_open2 \
  --enable-auto-tool-choice
```

For smaller hardware, **NotaAI** ships official quantized variants: [INT4 (87 GB)](https://huggingface.co/nota-ai/Solar-Open2-250B-Nota-INT4), [NVFP4](https://huggingface.co/nota-ai/Solar-Open2-250B-Nota-NVFP4), and [GlobalPruned INT4](https://huggingface.co/nota-ai/Solar-Open2-250B-Nota-INT4-GlobalPruned) that fit on fewer GPUs.

**Bottom line:** Solar Open 2 is the strongest open-weight agentic model to ship in 2026 so far, trading the raw reasoning ceiling of DeepSeek-V4 for better tool-use ergonomics and dramatically cheaper long-context deployment. Watch this space.
