## HF Trending Deep Dive: Nanbeige/Nanbeige4.2-3B

**Nanbeige4.2-3B** is a compact agentic language model from Nanbeige, released July 21, 2026 under Apache 2.0. At just 3B non-embedding parameters (~4B total), it punches far above its weight class by leveraging a **Looped Transformer** architecture — 22 transformer layers reused over 2 loops (44 effective layers), with 3072 hidden size, 48 attention heads, 8 KV heads for grouped-query attention, and a massive 262,144-token context window. The architecture also debuts three novel improvements — **LoopSplit**, **mHC with depth attention**, and **concatenated n-gram embeddings** — that Nanbeige has said will carry into their upcoming Nanbeige4.5 release. RoPE theta is set to 70M for long-context stability, and the model uses bfloat16 throughout. It's bilingual (English + Chinese) and packaged as a standard `transformers` model with custom `modeling_nanbeige.py` code baked in.

What makes Nanbeige4.2-3B genuinely impressive is its benchmark performance against models 2–4× its size. On SWE-Bench Verified it scores **63.6%** — beating Qwen3.5-9B (53.1%) and Gemma4-12B (44.2%) by wide margins. SWE-Bench Pro: **46.9%** vs Qwen3.5-9B's 33.8%. For reasoning, GPQA-Diamond hits **87.4%** (tops in its size class), HMMT-Feb-2026 reaches **82.8%**, and LiveCodeBench-V6 lands at **72.5%** — competitive with Gemma4-12B's 72.0% despite having ¼ the parameters. Training combined supervised fine-tuning across diverse real and synthetic agent environments with dual outcome+process reward signals during RL, using trajectory- and turn-level filtering. The model is designed as a **local personal assistant** — Nanbeige pitches it for daily office work, tool orchestration, and code-agent tasks running on consumer hardware, especially when paired with an agentic scaffold like OpenClaw. At 4B total parameters in bfloat16 (~8GB RAM), it's viable on laptops and edge devices, making it a compelling alternative for anyone who needs a capable local agent without cloud dependencies.
