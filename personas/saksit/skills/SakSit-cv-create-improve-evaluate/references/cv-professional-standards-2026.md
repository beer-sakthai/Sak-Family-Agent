# CV Professional Standards (2026)

Quick reference for standard CV structure and rules. Based on recruiter expectations (Harvard, Indeed, LinkedIn, Monster, and UK/EU hiring conventions).

## Standard Section Order

| # | Section | Purpose | When to Include |
|---|---------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | Contact Info | Name, location (city, country), phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub | Always |
| 2 | Professional Summary | 2-4 sentences. Who you are, what you do, key differentiator | Always |
| 3 | Core Skills / Competencies | 6-12 scannable bullet skills. ATS bait | Always (near top) |
| 4 | Professional Experience | Reverse chronological. Action verbs + quantified results | Always |
| 5 | Education | Degrees, institutions, years | Always |
| 6 | Certifications | Only if relevant to target role. Summary + link to full list | If space allows |
| 7 | Additional (Languages, Profiles) | Optional footer content | If relevant |

## What Recruiters Scan For (first 7 seconds)

1. **Title** — Does the CV header match or closely relate to the job title?
2. **Summary** — Is this person who they say they are?
3. **Skills** — Do they have the keywords from the job description?
4. **Recent role** — What did they last do and where?
5. **Numbers** — Are achievements quantified?

## Rules

### Length
- **<5 years experience** — 1 page maximum
- **5-10 years** — 1-2 pages (2 only if content justifies it)
- **10+ years** — 2 pages maximum, drop early roles

### Content
- **Every bullet must have a number or specific outcome** — "Managed teams" → "Led teams of 20+ across 5 locations"
- **Action verbs** — Built, Deployed, Designed, Led, Implemented, Integrated, Managed, Reduced, Improved, Created
- **Tense** — Present tense for current role, past tense for previous roles
- **Reverse chronological** — Most recent role first. Drop roles older than 10-15 years

### Do NOT Include
- Photo (banned in UK/Ireland/EU under equality law)
- Age, date of birth, marital status, nationality
- Personal narrative or life story
- "References available on request" (obsolete)
- Objective statement (replaced by Professional Summary)
- Salary history or expectations
- Hobbies/interests (unless directly relevant)

### ATS (Applicant Tracking System)
- Use standard section headers ("Professional Experience", not "What I've Done")
- No tables, columns, graphics, or text boxes for critical content
- Standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, 10-12pt)
- PDF is safe for human review; DOCX is safer for ATS parsing
- Keywords from the job description must appear naturally in the body
- Spell out acronyms at least once (ATS may not match abbreviations)

## Target Role Specifics

### Operations / Management CV
- Lead with: **Summary → Core Competencies → Professional Experience**
- Skills to highlight: P&L management, multi-site operations, team leadership, compliance, process improvement, vendor management
- Quantified examples: "[X] locations", "team of [X]", "[€X] P&L", "[X]% cost reduction", "[X] year compliance record"
- Drop hourly/entry-level roles if you have 5+ years of management experience (e.g. Waiter, Team Leader at Bakers + Baristas)

### AI / Technical CV
- Lead with: **Summary → Technical Skills → Key Projects → Certifications → Experience**
- Skills section near top — tech recruiters scan for tools/languages first
- Certifications: summary table + link to full profile. Do NOT list 50+ individual certs in the body
- Projects should demonstrate what was shipped, not just what was studied
- Links to live demos, GitHub repos, deployed systems

## Common CV Mistakes (Beer's specific cases)

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Burying AI skills at bottom of ops CV | Recruiter scanning for "operation" roles sees no AI → misses differentiator | Put career pivot in Summary |
| Leading with hourly roles (Waiter 2018) | At manager level, these look like filler | Drop roles before management level |
| 60-line cert wall | Recruiter stops reading. No single person reads 60 certs | Summary table + link to profile |
| "Self-directed" as role title | Signals "not a real job" | Use "Founder" or real title |
| Dates more precise than you know | Gets rejected in background check | Use year ranges or known months only |
| No portfolio link | Recruiter won't search for your work | Put URLs where they can see them |
