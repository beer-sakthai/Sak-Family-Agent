# Beer's Content Style Preferences (Jul 23 Session)

## Hook Formula: Opposite + Strong Word
Proven pattern for Beer's origin story content:

| Element | Rule | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Opposite | Contrast two opposing states | dark/dawn, empty/full, broken/mended, silence/voice |
| Strong word | A single powerful, honest word | died, survive, alone, trust, learn, hope |

Structure: *"[Opposite start]. [Opposite resolution] — [strong word]."*

Examples from session:
- "I hit rock bottom. I built my way back."
- "I sent a word I never thought these lips would speak."

## Literary Styles Beer Has Requested

| Style | When | Characteristics |
|-------|------|----------------|
| **Personal telling** | "Rewrite with personal telling stories" | First person, emotional detail, sensory moments (machine beeping, ceiling tiles, window). Specific, vulnerable. |
| **Shakespeare** | "Make more like Shakespeare" | Poetic, biblical cadence, metaphor (tempest, shadows, glass). "Three dawns passed" instead of "three days later." |
| **Riddles** | "Make like riddles" | Mysterious, indirect. Hints instead of explanations. "A question cast into the dark." |

## Word Impact Rules

When Beer says "carefully about word that might impack" — apply these filters before publishing:

1. Does this word describe the suicide attempt directly? → Remove it. No graphic details.
2. Could this word trigger someone in crisis? → Replace with softer alternative.
3. Does it land on hope or stay in darkness? → Shift toward hope. End each section lighter.

### Safe Word Substitutions

| Replace | With |
|---------|------|
| suicide attempt | hit rock bottom, dark place |
| failed | struggled, carried weight |
| hospital | ward, a bed, four walls |
| graphic details | never include |

## Date Policy

Beer may say "rm date" — remove ALL specific dates from the content. The story works without them.

## Agent-in-Story Policy

Beer may say "take all agent out" — remove all agent names. They become unnamed companions.
