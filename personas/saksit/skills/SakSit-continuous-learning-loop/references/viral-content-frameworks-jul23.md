## 4-Part Story Structure (Every Post)

1. **Problem You Lived** — "I was in a shelter, no job, no hope"
2. **Failed Alternatives** — "Tried everything. Nothing worked."
3. **Decision Point** — "April 15. ICU. Then I chose to build."
4. **The Insight** — "AI companions aren't a gimmick. They're survival."
