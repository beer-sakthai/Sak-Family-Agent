## Source 2: Instagram & TikTok Algorithm 2026 — Consistency Beats Viral

**URL:** `https://truescho.com/en/blog/instagram-tiktok-algorithm-consistency-2026`
**Accessed:** 2026-07-25 via terminal curl

### Key Quote (the headline finding)

> "A single viral post is now worth roughly 1/4 of what it was worth in 2023, while a steady weekly cadence is worth 4x more."

### Algorithm Ranking Factors (per Adam Mosseri, April 2026)

As confirmed by Mosseri, Instagram ranks content using:
- **Interest** – what you've engaged with before
- **Recency** – newer posts get more reach
- **Relationship** – DM and save signals
- **Frequency** – how often each user opens Instagram

Likes were officially down-weighted as a ranking signal.

### Instagram Engagement Drop (-26%)

Buffer's 2026 report attributes the drop to three factors:
1. Likes being down-weighted as a ranking signal
2. Reels supply tripling while user attention stayed flat
3. Algorithm now requiring sustained weekly signals instead of rewarding individual posts

### Topic Penalty

> "Accounts that post across 3+ unrelated topics suffer a -45% reach penalty (Socialync 2026 analysis)."

### 5-3-1 Daily Engagement Rule

Standard daily protocol from late 2025 that produces measurable algorithm response within 7–10 days:
- **5** — Reply to 5 comments within 60 minutes of publishing
- **3** — Engage genuinely (comment, save, share) on 3 creators' posts
- **1** — Send 1 meaningful DM to someone in your niche

## Synthesis for Beer

The three sources converge on the same 2026 reality:

1. **Follower counts are decorative.** The algorithm distributes content based on real-time interest signals, not follower loyalty. A 50-follower account with tight topical focus can outperform a 50K-follower account that posts variably.

2. **Topic discipline is the new growth lever.** The -45% TikTok penalty for 3+ unrelated topics is the canary in the coal mine — all platforms are converging on topical authority as a primary discovery signal. Every post outside your 2–3 chosen lanes directly harms future reach.

3. **Consistency compounds, virality doesn't.** The 4× devaluation of viral posts means the algorithm now reads your overall posting trajectory, not individual spikes. A steady 3–5 posts/week on the same topics for 8–12 weeks builds more discoverability than a single viral post ever could.
