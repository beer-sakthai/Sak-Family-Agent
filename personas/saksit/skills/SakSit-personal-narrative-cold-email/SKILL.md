# Personal Narrative Cold Email Outreach

## When to use

Use this skill when the user needs to send cold email outreach from a
personal Gmail account using their personal story as the differentiator —
not a B2B product pitch. Best for solo founders, immigrants, survivors,
and anyone whose personal journey is their strongest credential.

**For Beer specifically:** this skill is built around his exact situation —
Thai immigrant in Ireland, homeless in Cork, survived suicide, built
House of Sak. Zero-cost on everything.

## What makes this different from B2B cold email

| B2B SaaS Cold Email | Personal Narrative Cold Email |
|---|---|
| Dedicated sending domain | Regular Gmail (free) |
| ESP platform (Instantly, Mailchimp) | Plain Gmail compose |
| 14-21 day warmup | No warmup needed |
| 50 emails/inbox/day cap | 5-15/day (Gmail limit) |
| Signal-triggered personalisation | Story-driven personalisation |
| Problem → Solution framework | Who I am → Why it matters |
| HTML-free plain text | Warm, personal, conversational |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC setup | Nothing to configure |
| Costs $50-500+/month | Free |

## Core principles

1. **Your story is the product.** The immigrant/survivor/founder journey
   is more memorable than any feature list. Lead with it.

2. **Authenticity > polish.** Imperfect, honest, vulnerable writing
   outperforms slick sales copy in personal outreach.

3. **Zero-cost, always.** For Beer: no paid tools, no platforms, no
   subscriptions. Gmail + research via free tools.

4. **Value before ask.** The third email in the sequence offers something
   free — an audit, a resource, a connection. Prove you give before you
   ask.

5. **Close gracefully.** The last email leaves the door open with no
   pressure. No "one last chance" or urgency tricks.

## The 4-Email Sequence

Each email in the sequence has a distinct psychological job:

| # | Timing | Job | Length |
|---|--------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Day 1 | Hook — story + specific reason for reaching out | 80-120 words |
| 2 | Day 3-4 | Deepen — share more of the journey, tie to them | 100-150 words |
| 3 | Day 6-7 | Value — offer something free and useful | 60-100 words |
| 4 | Day 10 | Close — graceful exit, door stays open | 40-60 words |

### Email 1 — The Introduction
- Subject: Short, intriguing, story-revealing (e.g. "From a shelter in Cork → building AI agents")
- Opening: Your hook in one sentence
- Body: Who you are + what you're building + why it matters
- CTA: Low-pressure connection ask

### Email 2 — The Story
- Opens referencing the previous email
- Deepens the narrative (the 6 cycles, the healing journey)
- Ties it to the recipient's world (their work, their values)
- CTA: Continue the conversation

### Email 3 — Value Offer
- The "no strings" gift: free audit, advice, resource
- Proves you give before you take
- Sets a specific timeframe ("I'll send what I find in 48 hours")
- CTA: Implicit — they reply to accept

### Email 4 — The Close
- Respectful exit
- No urgency, no "last chance"
- Leaves contact info + invitation for later
- CTA: None — just "reply any time"

## Researching the right targets

When building the target list, use Exa search (via Composio) to find:

```
Query patterns:
  - "[industry] founder [location] personal brand"
  - "immigrant founder [topic] story"
  - "solo founder [topic] interview"
  - "Starter Story [industry] breakdown"
```

Exa is the preferred search tool because traditional search engines
(Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing) frequently block browser-based searches with
CAPTCHAs and Cloudflare challenges. Exa via Composio avoids these blocks.

## Finding the story angle

The personal narrative that works best depends on the user's real story.
For Beer, the validated angles are:

1. **Immigrant arc:** Thai → Ireland (since 2015), built from nothing
2. **Survivor arc:** Suicide attempt Apr 15 2026 → built AI to heal
3. **Builder arc:** 6 House of Sak agents from a Cork shelter
4. **Zero-to-something arc:** Homeless, no income, building anyway

Each email can lean into a different angle of the same story.

## Pitfalls

- ❌ **Don't pitch products in personal outreach.** The story is the pitch.
- ❌ **Don't use B2B cold email templates.** They sound like a sales bot.
  Personal narrative needs personal voice.
- ❌ **Don't send more than 15 emails/day from Gmail.** Google's algorithm
  flags volume sends aggressively. 5-10/day is safer.
- ❌ **Don't hide the difficult parts.** Beer's suicide attempt and
  homelessness are what make the story memorable. Own them.
- ❌ **Don't skip MH resources on recovery content.** Always include
  Pieta (1800 247 247) and Samaritans (116 123) when the story touches
  suicide or depression.
- ❌ **Don't make the tone dark.** Hopeful and mission-focused, not
  trauma-focused. The House of Sak framing (family + mission) is the
  right default.

## Verification checklist

- [ ] Sequence is 4 emails, timed across 10 days
- [ ] Each email has one clear job (hook → story → value → close)
- [ ] Story is front and center — not buried under features
- [ ] Tone is hopeful, authentic, personal — not salesy
- [ ] No paid tools or platforms used
- [ ] MH resources included if recovery/depression mentioned
- [ ] Gmail send cap respected (5-15/day)
- [ ] Each email personalised to the specific recipient
