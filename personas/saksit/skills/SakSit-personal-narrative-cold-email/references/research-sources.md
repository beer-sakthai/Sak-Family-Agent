## Notes for future sessions

- **Exa search** via Composio worked well when Google/DuckDuckGo/Bing were
  all blocked by bot detection. Prefer Exa for web research in this skill.
- **Starter Story** has the best broke-to-built narrative case studies.
  Their formula: honest revenue numbers + personal struggle + specific
  timeline. Good structural inspiration.
- **Immigrant founder stories** cluster around common themes: leaving
  everything behind, building from nothing, using outsider perspective
  as advantage. Beer's story has all three plus a survival angle.
