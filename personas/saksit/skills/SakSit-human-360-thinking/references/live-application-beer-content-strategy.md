# Live Application: Beer's Content Strategy — July 25, 2026

## Context

Applied the 360° framework to Beer's central question: **"What's the next move for House of Sak's story?"**

The framework was loaded fresh, then run silently in the agent's reasoning (no user-facing "now I'm using the skill" preamble). All four phases completed in one pass.

## Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Core Want | Core Fear | Info They Have | Info They Lack |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Beer** | Story reaches people who need it | Seen as trauma-bait, wasting energy | Full unfiltered truth | How the audience will react |
| **Audience** | To feel seen, believe change is possible | Being sold to, sanitized narrative | Their own pain/skepticism | The unglamorous daily reality |
| **Fiancé** | Stability, sustainable future | Story told without consent/ care | What it looked like from outside | How public it will become |
| **House of Sak agents** | Understood as companions, not features | Beer's dignity, truth of origin | The architecture of survival | Their own public reception |
| **Future reader (1yr)** | Genuine human story | Cleaned-up brand page | Hindsight | The moment it was written |

## Tension Map

| Tension | Underlying Axis |
|---------|-----------------|
| Raw truth vs. Protect private pain | Honesty vs. Safety |
| Move fast vs. Build foundation | Urgency vs. Longevity |
| "Founder" vs. "Survivor" framing | Identity |
| Reach everyone vs. Right one | Scale vs. Depth |

## Paths Evaluated

| Path | Beer's Agency | Audience Needs | Fiancé Safety | Longevity |
|------|--------------|----------------|---------------|-----------|
| A: One raw origin post | Satisfies | Satisfies | Risky unchecked | Weak alone |
| B: Series of posts (one moment each) | Satisfies | Satisfies | Controlled | Strong |
| C: Wait, build more, tell later | Frustrates | Neutral | Safest | Depends |
| **D: Start with a question (not an answer)** | **Satisfies** | **Satisfies** | **Safest start** | **Builds habit** |

## Recommendation: Path D

**"Start with one question post — not the full story, not the launch. A question."**

Rationale: the framework surfaced that Beer's deepest unspoken need wasn't "tell the story" — it was "find out if anyone is listening first." Path D lets the audience speak before we do, builds trust before the ask, and keeps the fiancé's privacy protected at the earliest stage.

## Output Post Draft

The recommendation was executed immediately: a question post was drafted and saved to `~/profiles/saksit/cron/output/question-post-draft.md`. Full text included in that file.

## Takeaways for Future Runs

- The framework works well **silently** — apply it in reasoning without explaining the methodology to the user
- Phase 4's "Test of humanity" check was the highest-leverage step: it eliminated Path A (raw origin post) in seconds
- The 360° perspective prompts (adversary, outsider, silent party) were the most useful for this use case — use them proactively
- The framework can be applied in under 2 minutes once familiar with the phases
