## Trial Reels — Key Detail

> "Trial Reels let you test content with non-followers. This feature allows creators to show Reels to people who don't follow them first, gathering engagement data before deciding whether to push content to their existing audience."

**How it works per the article:**
1. Post a Reel as a "Trial" — Instagram shows it to non-followers first
2. Engagement is measured (views, shares, likes, completion rate)
3. If strong → the Reel gets pushed to your existing followers automatically
4. If weak → you can delete it silently without your audience ever seeing it

**Implication:** Creators can run 3-4 content experiments per week with zero audience risk. This is a game-changer for accounts under 10K followers who can't afford to lose engagement rate with underperforming posts.

## Instagram Collabs — Key Detail

> "Instagram Collabs posts (with co-shared authorship) let you reach new audiences by partnering with other creators. Instagram now allows up to three collaborators on a single post."

**Mechanics:**
- Up to 3 co-authors on a single Feed post or Reel
- Views, likes, and comments are shared across all co-authors
- The post appears on each co-author's profile grid
- Shows up in each co-author's followers' feeds

**Implication:** This is the highest-leverage organic tactic because it bypasses the cold-start algorithm problem entirely — content lands directly in each collaborator's audience without needing to be discovered through Explore or Reels.
