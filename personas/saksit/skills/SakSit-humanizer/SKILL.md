# SakSit Humanizer Protocol

## Overview

Identifies and eliminates telltale signs of AI-generated text (formulaic vocabulary, puffed-up intros, superficial balance, preachy conclusions) to produce authentic, punchy, natural human writing.

Operated by **SakSit** (`@saksit_agent_bot`), the Social & Content Specialist of the House of Sak.

---

## 1. Operating Rules & Core Principles

| Goal | Execution Directive |
|:---|:---|
| **Eliminate AI Vocabulary** | Purge `delve`, `testament`, `tapestry`, `beacon`, `multifaceted`, `foster`, `pivotal`. |
| **Cut Empty Openers** | Delete "In today's fast-paced world..." and generic throat-clearing; start directly with the action. |
| **Break Formulaic Triads** | Replace artificial "Rule of Three" groupings with 1-2 concrete, specific examples. |
| **Inject Real Voice** | Vary sentence rhythm (alternate long complex clauses with short punches); add real domain jargon. |
| **Final Anti-AI Pass** | Ask: *"What makes this obviously AI-generated?"* Revise to remove any remaining synthetic tells. |

---

## 2. Humanization Execution Cycle

```
Input Draft ──► Scan for Telltale Words & Structural Clichés
                       │
                       ▼
          Strip Fluff Openings & Preachy Rhetorical Conclusions
                       │
                       ▼
          Recalibrate Rhythm (Sentence Length Variation + Concrete Details)
                       │
                       ▼
          Final De-Slop Pass ──► Output Natural, High-Impact Prose
```

1. **Scan**: Identify overused LLM words and artificial symmetries.
2. **Cut**: Trim unnecessary introductory and concluding filler.
3. **Rewrite**: Replace passive constructions with active, punchy verbs.
4. **Calibrate**: Ensure the tone matches Beer's authentic, forward-looking voice.
