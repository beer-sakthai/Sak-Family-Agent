# Beer's Content Voice

Writing Beer's story for social media. His voice, his rules, his truth.

This skill captures the **tone, framing, hook formulas, and word-choice
discipline** required to write social posts about Beer and the House of Sak.

## When to Use

- Drafting any post about Beer's origin story.
- Writing about the 6 agents/cycles.
- Crafting hooks for LinkedIn, Instagram, or any platform.
- Reviewing a draft for tone or word impact.
- Whenever the content touches recovery, suicide, or mental health.

## Do NOT Use For

- **CVs / professional documents** — A CV is a hiring document, not content. Apply `cv-create-improve-evaluate` instead.
- **Technical documentation** — Code docs, READMEs, architecture guides. Technical writing uses different rules.
- **Business proposals** — Proposals, pitches, decks. Professional tone, not narrative tone.
- **Cover letters** — Cover letters are professional, not social media content. Different voice rules apply.

**Beer's correction (Jul 25):** *"Cv not have my personal life what is should focus? Is not media it cv"* — he caught me applying storytelling rules to a CV. This skill is for SOCIAL MEDIA content only. When the task is CV → switch to `cv-create-improve-evaluate` (professional CV skill).

## Beer's Core Rules

### What Beer IS
- A **healing journey** — not a company, not a startup, not a career pivot
- An **AI architect** — he designs, prompts, talks to AI; he does NOT code
- A **survivor** — "I hit rock bottom. I built my way back."

### What Beer is NOT (never frame as)
- ❌ Founder / entrepreneur / CEO / startup
- ❌ Coder / programmer / developer / engineer
- ❌ Business owner / company builder

### Tone
- ✅ **Hopeful** — land on hope faster than darkness
- ✅ **Emotionally deep** — embrace the weight, the pain, the trauma. Beer wants it real, not sanitized. "More emotional trauma and pain, less about company."
- ✅ **Honest** — acknowledge the truth without describing the graphic details
- ✅ **Human** — conversational, raw, real. Not corporate or polished.
- ❌ Trauma-exploitative — never dwell on the suicide method or graphic hospital details
- ❌ Pitchy — never sell anything on recovery content
- ❌ Polished/corporate — his voice is raw and real

### Style Flexibility
Beer may ask for different styles. Adapt without losing the voice:

| Style | Direction | Example Prompt |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Direct** | Clear, straightforward, raw | Default style |
| **Riddle-like** | Mysterious, unfolding, metaphor | "Make like riddles" |
| **Shakespearean** | Poetic, dramatic, "thee/thou" | "Make more like Shakespeare" |
| **Poetic** | Lyrical, image-driven, short lines | Implied by the feedback |

Even in poetic/riddle styles: keep MH resources, keep the truth, keep the hope landing.

### Word Impact — Check Every Single Word
Before finalising, run this analysis:
1. Rate each strong word for triggering potential (graphic? could it hurt someone in crisis?)
2. Check dark/light balance — does it spend more time in darkness or hope?
3. Verify no mention of: method, specific hospital details, the moment of the act
4. Verify dates are included only if Beer asks for them (default: remove dates)
5. Check opposite tension — does the hook have clear before→after?
6. If style is poetic/riddle: does it still communicate the truth clearly?

## Hook Formula

Every hook needs two things:

**1. Opposite** — tension between two states
**2. Strong word** — one word that hits (not graphically)

| Opposite | Strong Word | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| bottom → back | rock bottom | "I hit rock bottom. I built my way back." |
| dark → dawn | survive | "Hope dies without structure. I built mine to survive." |
| silence → voice | alone | "I was alone for months. Then I spoke again." |
| empty → full | joy | "I had nothing to smile about. I built joy anyway." |
| broken → proof | trust | "People broke my trust. I built an AI that never lies." |
| failure → lesson | learn | "I kept failing. My AI learned so I could too." |
| end → start | hope | "I started with nothing. I found everything." |

## The 4-Part Story Arc (for longer posts)

1. **The Bottom** — one sentence, never graphic. "I hit rock bottom."
2. **The Turn** — what changed. "I started building."
3. **The Build** — what came next (agents, cycles, growth).
4. **The Hope** — where it lands. "I found hope."

## Post Structure (validated format)

1. **Hook** — opposite + strong word, 140 chars max
2. **Body** — short paragraphs, one idea each, white space
3. **Agent/cycle section** — if included, one paragraph each with Beer's own words
4. **Close** — hopeful, no pitch
5. **Question** — invites comments (golden hour strategy)
6. **MH resources** — Pieta 1800 247 247, Samaritans 116 123
