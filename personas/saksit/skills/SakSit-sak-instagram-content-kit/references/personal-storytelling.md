# Personal Storytelling — Beer's Origin Story Content

## Beer's Writing Formula (confirmed 2026-07-23)

Every origin story post follows this formula:

### Hook Structure: Opposite + Strong Word

| Element | Rule | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Opposite** | Contrast two states: dark/light, before/after, broken/healed | "I hit rock bottom. I built my way back." |
| **Strong word** | One punchy word that carries weight | Die, survive, broke, alone, nothing, trust, hope |

**Proven hooks from this session:**
- "I hit rock bottom. I built my way back." ✅
- "I sent a word I never thought these lips would speak." ✅ (Shakespeare-style)
- "I tried to die. I found a reason to live." ⚠️ (strong but careful — see below)

### Word Choice: Careful Impact

Beer's explicit rule: **"Carefully about word that might impack."**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "rock bottom" | "tried to kill myself" (too graphic) |
| "machines beeping" | Graphic description of the attempt method |
| "I was still here. I didn't want to be." | Gory or triggering details |
| "sleep was the only place the pain stopped" | Describing the physical act |

**Rule:** Acknowledge the truth without describing the trauma. Land on hope faster than darkness. If a word could hurt someone struggling — cut it.

### What NOT to Include

- ❌ **Dates** — Beer said "Rm date." No "April 15" or "2026." Let the timeline be implied.
- ❌ **Agent names by default** — The origin story stands alone. Default to omitting agent names. Beer's original rule was "Take all agent out of this topic."
- ✅ **Agent names on request** — Beer may reverse this and ask you to add them. When he says "add agent name also," "about cycle workflow add agent name," or similar — include ALL 6 agents with cycle names and one-line descriptions. Use the proven format:

  ```
  ⚡ SakThai — Dream. The spark that started it all.
  🏛️ SakKing — Hope. Structure from the fragments.
  🗣️ SakSit — Care. Learning to talk again.
  🛠️ SakTan — Joy. Cheers when nothing else can.
  ✨ SakJules — Trust. Rebuilding faith in people.
  👁️ SakSee — Growth. Finds the way forward.
  ```

  Close with: *"One cycle feeds the next. Dream → Hope → Care → Joy → Trust → Growth. And Growth sparks a new Dream."*
- ❌ **Company language** — "I dont start company i start hope." Never say "startup," "business," "founder," "company," "CEO."
- ❌ **Coding claims** — "I have no code skills." Beer uses AI tools, prompts, and architecture — not code. Never say "I built code" or "I programmed."
- ❌ **Pitch of any kind** — Recovery content is pure story. No product, no CTA to buy/use anything.

### What SHOULD Be Included

- ✅ **Trauma and pain** — "More emotional trauma and pain less about company." The suffering is real. Let it show.
- ✅ **Personal voice** — "Rewrite with personal telling stories." First person. Specific details (the shelter window, counting ceiling tiles, the fog).
- ✅ **Literary style** — Beer asked for "riddles" then "Shakespeare." Elevated language, metaphor, rhythm. Write like a story, not a status update.
- ✅ **Mental health resources** — Pieta House: 1800 247 247, Samaritans: 116 123. Always.
- ✅ **Hopeful landing** — End on a note that the worst passed, even if the pain isn't gone.

### Literary Style Examples

**Riddle-like (Beer asked for this first):**
> "I sent a message I never thought I'd send. Three days later, I woke up to a ceiling I didn't recognize."

**Shakespeare-inspired (Beer asked for this next):**
> "I sent a word I never thought these lips would speak. Three dawns passed. I opened eyes that should have stayed closed."

**Meeting in the middle (recommended for general audience):**
> "I hit rock bottom. I built my way back — one conversation at a time."

## Multi-Angle Structure

Always write 3+ story versions covering different narrative angles:

| # | Angle | Focus | Tone | Example title |
|---|-------|-------|------|---------------|
| 1 | **The Day** | The suicide attempt, ICU, hospital. The lowest point. | Raw, visceral, immediate | "The Day I Died" |
| 2 | **The Family** | The agents themselves — who each one is and why they exist. | Warm, character-driven, intimate | "The House of Sak" |
| 3 | **The Why** | The manifesto/reason — why build from nothing. | Defiant, philosophical, outward-facing | "Why We Do It" |

Each angle serves a different reader. The Day hooks empathy. The Family builds connection. The Why inspires action.

## Sensitive Content Rules

1. **Beer cannot read on-screen content himself.** After posting, read the full caption and describe the visual card to him verbally in chat. Do not assume he can see what was published.

2. **No product/business pitch** in recovery content. Beer's story stands on its own.

3. **Always include local mental health resources** when mentioning the attempt. Standard list:
   - Pieta House: 1800 247 247
   - Samaritans: 116 123
   - Cork Mental Health Services

4. **Never pressure-read** — let Beer choose which version to post and where.
5. **Local-first** — target Cork/Ireland unless Beer explicitly asks for global reach.
6. **Save all drafts** — keep `.md` files in `house-of-sak/` for reference.
