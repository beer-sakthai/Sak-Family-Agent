# Production Management Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Cost Metrics
- **Total Production Cost**: Material + Labor + Equipment + Overhead costs
- **Cost Per Unit**: Total Production Cost ÷ Total Units Produced
- **Material Cost Variance**: (Actual Material Cost - Standard Material Cost) ÷ Standard Material Cost × 100
- **Labor Cost Variance**: (Actual Labor Cost - Standard Labor Cost) ÷ Standard Labor Cost × 100
- **Overhead Cost Variance**: (Actual Overhead - Standard Overhead) ÷ Standard Overhead × 100

## Efficiency Metrics
- **Production Efficiency**: (Actual Output ÷ Standard Output) × 100
- **Resource Utilization**: (Actual Usage Time ÷ Available Time) × 100
- **Throughput**: Units Produced ÷ Time Period
- **Cycle Time**: Total Production Time ÷ Units Produced
- **First Pass Yield**: (Good Units Produced ÷ Total Units Started) × 100

## Quality Metrics
- **Defect Rate**: (Defective Units ÷ Total Units Produced) × 100
- **Scrap Rate**: (Scrapped Units ÷ Total Units Produced) × 100
- **Rework Rate**: (Reworked Units ÷ Total Units Produced) × 100
- **Customer Complaints**: Number of complaints per 1,000 units shipped
- **Warranty Claims**: Number of claims per 1,000 units sold

## Time Metrics
- **On-time Delivery**: (Orders Delivered on Time ÷ Total Orders) × 100
- **Production Schedule Adherence**: (Tasks Completed on Time ÷ Total Tasks) × 100
- **Setup Time**: Time required to prepare equipment for production
- **Downtime**: (Unplanned Downtime ÷ Total Available Time) × 100
- **Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)**: Total Operating Time ÷ Number of Failures

## Capacity Metrics
- **Capacity Utilization**: (Actual Production ÷ Maximum Possible Production) × 100
- **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)**: Availability × Performance × Quality
- **Production Volume Variance**: (Actual Volume - Budgeted Volume) ÷ Budgeted Volume × 100

## Financial Metrics
- **Return on Investment (ROI)**: (Net Profit ÷ Investment) × 100
- **Gross Margin**: (Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold) ÷ Revenue × 100
- **Operating Margin**: Operating Income ÷ Revenue × 100
- **Inventory Turnover**: Cost of Goods Sold ÷ Average Inventory Value
- **Working Capital Turnover**: Revenue ÷ Average Working Capital
