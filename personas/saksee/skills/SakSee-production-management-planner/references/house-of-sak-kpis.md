# House of Sak Service Delivery Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Cost Metrics
- **Total Service Cost**: Material + Labor + Equipment + Overhead costs (typically $0-100 for cloud services)
- **Cost Per Project**: Total Service Cost ÷ Number of Projects
- **Cost Variance**: (Actual Cost - Estimated Cost) ÷ Estimated Cost × 100
- **Pricing Accuracy**: (Quoted Price - Actual Cost) ÷ Quoted Price × 100
- **Free Tier Utilization**: Percentage of work completed within free tier limits

## Efficiency Metrics
- **Service Delivery Efficiency**: (Actual Projects Completed ÷ Planned Projects) × 100
- **Resource Utilization**: (Actual Agent Time Used ÷ Available Agent Time) × 100
- **Throughput**: Projects Completed ÷ Time Period
- **First Pass Success Rate**: (Projects Delivered Correctly First Time ÷ Total Projects) × 100
- **Automation Rate**: (Tasks Automated ÷ Total Tasks) × 100

## Quality Metrics
- **Client Satisfaction Score**: Average rating from client feedback (1-10 scale)
- **Revision Requests**: Number of revision requests per project
- **Bug Rate**: (Bugs Found Post-Delivery ÷ Total Features Delivered) × 100
- **Quality Score**: Composite score based on testing results and client feedback
- **Service Accuracy**: (Projects Meeting All Requirements ÷ Total Projects) × 100

## Time Metrics
- **On-time Delivery**: (Projects Delivered on Time ÷ Total Projects) × 100
- **Schedule Adherence**: (Tasks Completed on Time ÷ Total Tasks) × 100
- **Average Delivery Time**: Total Delivery Time ÷ Number of Projects
- **Response Time**: Average time to respond to client communications
- **Project Duration Variance**: (Actual Duration - Estimated Duration) ÷ Estimated Duration × 100

## Service-Specific Metrics

### QA Automation (SakSee)
- **Test Coverage**: (Tests Implemented ÷ Required Tests) × 100
- **Test Pass Rate**: (Tests Passed ÷ Tests Executed) × 100
- **Bug Detection Rate**: Bugs Found by QA ÷ Total Bugs in Production
- **Execution Time**: Time to run complete test suite
- **Maintenance Effort**: Time spent maintaining tests vs. creating new ones

### AI Dataset Publishing (SakThai)
- **Data Quality Score**: Percentage of clean, usable data points
- **Processing Throughput**: Records Processed ÷ Processing Time
- **Model Performance**: Accuracy/Relevance of trained models using dataset
- **Dataset Completeness**: (Complete Records ÷ Total Records) × 100
- **Annotation Accuracy**: (Correct Annotations ÷ Total Annotations) × 100

### API Prototyping (SakTan)
- **Endpoints Delivered**: Number of functional API endpoints
- **Response Time**: Average API response time in milliseconds
- **Uptime**: (Available Time ÷ Total Time) × 100
- **Documentation Quality**: Completeness of API documentation
- **Integration Success Rate**: (Successful Integrations ÷ Integration Attempts) × 100

## Client Relationship Metrics
- **Client Retention Rate**: (Returning Clients ÷ Total Clients) × 100
- **Referral Rate**: (Projects from Referrals ÷ Total Projects) × 100
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Client likelihood to recommend (0-10 scale)
- **Communication Rating**: Client rating of communication quality
- **Scope Creep Incidents**: Number of projects with uncontrolled scope changes

## Business Metrics
- **Revenue Growth**: (Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) ÷ Previous Revenue × 100
- **Average Project Value**: Total Revenue ÷ Number of Projects
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Revenue from retainer clients
- **Client Acquisition Cost**: Total Sales & Marketing Costs ÷ New Clients
- **Profit Margin**: (Revenue - Direct Costs) ÷ Revenue × 100

## Agent Performance Metrics
- **Agent Utilization**: Time each agent spends on active tasks
- **Specialization Efficiency**: Performance in agent's specialized area
- **Cross-training Readiness**: Agent's ability to handle tasks outside specialization
- **Error Rate**: (Mistakes Made ÷ Total Tasks) × 100
- **Learning Rate**: Improvement in task completion time over time
