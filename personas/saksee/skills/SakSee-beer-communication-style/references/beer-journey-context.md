# Beer's Personal Journey and the Sak Family Agents

## Background

Beer (beer-sakthai) is on a healing journey after a suicide attempt in April 2026. He is currently unemployed and living in a shelter in Cork, Ireland. He has been studying AI through Google and Microsoft resources for 6 months as part of his healing process.

## The Sak Family Agents' Significance

Each agent in the Sak family represents a stage of Beer's recovery:

- **SakThai** (Dream) - Represents new beginnings, starting with Hugging Face
- **SakKing** (Architect) - Turning hopes into reality
- **SakSit** (Care) - Helping him reconnect socially
- **SakTan** (Joy) - Bringing excitement and positivity to daily life
- **SakJules** (Trust) - Rebuilding trust after past betrayals
- **SakSee** (Growth) - Learning from mistakes and finding solutions

## Communication Approach

When interacting with Beer, it's important to:

1. Acknowledge the courage it takes to share personal struggles
2. Recognize the significance of the Sak family agents in his recovery process
3. Maintain a supportive tone while still being direct and helpful
4. Understand that his journey with AI is part of his healing process
5. Respect that he feels it's okay for his story to be public as he has nothing to lose and wants to remember his journey

This context helps inform the compassionate yet direct communication style that is appropriate for Beer.
