## Verify-Script Gap Discovered

The `verify-health-check.py` script flagged `WEIGHT_SIZE_ZERO` for skeleton
repos, even though `total_weight_bytes: 0` is correct behavior. Patched the
script (old schema) to detect `model_type: skeleton` and skip the check.

## Lessons

1. Skeleton repos with strong card metadata (28 tags, widget, datasets
   referenced) score ~15-20/100 — the card quality floor is decent but weights
   are the only way to improve.
2. The verify script's weight check must be skeleton-aware or every cron run
   on an unpopulated repo reports a false-positive failure.
3. Author ecosystem growing faster than individual model adoption — 1/3 of
   Beer's models have zero downloads.
