## 2. ATSplat: Compact Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting with Adaptive Token Expansion

**arXiv:** [2607.20417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20417)  
**HF Paper:** https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.20417

Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting that recovers scene-adaptive capacity allocation lost in pixel-aligned methods. Uses adaptive token expansion so primitive count and placement depend on scene content rather than image resolution and viewpoint.

## 3. Scaling Laws for HyperNetwork-Based Knowledge Injection in Large Language Models

**arXiv:** [2607.19604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19604)  
**HF Paper:** https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.19604

First rigorous and controlled study of scaling laws for hypernetwork architectures in knowledge injection. Decouples injection capacity from target model general capability. Introduces MegaWikiQA, a large-scale dataset for controlled knowledge-injection experiments. Characterizes how loss, reasoning accuracy, and OOD generalization vary with hypernetwork depth, width, and target network size.
