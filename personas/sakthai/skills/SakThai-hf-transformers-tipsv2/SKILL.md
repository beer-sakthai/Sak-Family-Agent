## Overview

TIPSv2 (Text-Image Pre-training with Spatial awareness v2) is a family of
contrastive vision-language encoders from Google DeepMind, added in
Transformers v5.14.0 (July 2026). It excels at patch-text alignment — matching
individual image patches to corresponding text concepts — enabling strong
performance on zero-shot classification, retrieval, depth estimation, and
segmentation.
