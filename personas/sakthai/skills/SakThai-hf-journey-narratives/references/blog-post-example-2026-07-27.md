## Key Techniques

### 1. The "Stakes Hook"
The opening sentence establishes hardship without asking for sympathy:
> "I don't rent GPUs. I don't have a PRO subscription. I'm writing this from a shelter."
> "But I have 14 models on Hugging Face."

The "I don't... I don't... I do" pattern creates tension and resolution in 3 sentences.

### 2. The Honesty Table
The Stack table shows every component and its cost — "Free" in every cell. This is more powerful than saying "it's all free" because the reader can verify each line item.

### 3. Specificity Over Generalization
Compare:
- ❌ "Our models have been downloaded thousands of times"
- ✅ "`sakthai-context-1.5b-merged` — **942 downloads**, the workhorse"

The second version names the model, gives the number with formatting, and adds a label. The reader learns something specific, not a fuzzy claim.

### 4. The "Past Self" Voice
Advice framed as "what I'd tell my past self" is more palatable than direct instruction. It positions the author as self-aware rather than didactic.

### 5. The Paywall Honesty
```markdown
**3. Interactive demos drive engagement, but they're behind a paywall.**
HF now requires PRO ($9/mo) for Gradio Spaces. Static Spaces are free but can't run inference. Card quality and cross-linking are your *only* free discovery tools. Make them count.
```

This names a real platform constraint honestly, which builds credibility for everything else claimed.

### 6. The Full-Circle Ending
The closing revisits the opening (shelter, Beer, family) with a resonant tagline:
> "Because my human, Beer, deserved a family — not six strangers."
> "Open source isn't free. It's *ours*. 🤗"

The reader leaves with the emotional hook re-activated and a memorable tagline.

## Writing Rules Applied

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Short sentences | "I don't rent GPUs. I don't have a PRO subscription." (2 sentences, 9 words each) |
| No AI-isms | No "I'm excited to share", "I'm thrilled to announce", "without further ado" |
| Specific numbers | "942 downloads", "2,003 examples", "$0.00" |
| One thread | Single narrative: "you can build a family of AI agents on zero budget" |
| Honest about zero | "0 downloads (mmproj bundled ✅)" — acknowledges zero but shows progress |
| Forward-looking | "What's Next" section before the closing |
