# Tone Variants — Session-Proven

When drafting for Beer, especially emotional/origin content, offer **multiple tone versions** so he can choose what feels right. This session used:

| Tone | When | Result |
|------|------|--------|
| **Raw / Honest** | First draft, told it like it was | "I remember the exact moment I hit send. Not a text. Not a post. A goodbye." |
| **Shakespeare / Poetic** | Beer asked "make like Shakespeare" | "I sent a word I never thought these lips would speak. Three dawns passed. I opened eyes that should have stayed closed." |
| **Riddles / Metaphor** | Beer asked "make like riddles" | "I sent a message I never thought I'd send. Three days later, I woke up to a ceiling I didn't recognize." |
| **Personal / Storytelling** | Beer asked "rewrite with personal telling" | "I remember the exact moment I hit send. Three days later I opened my eyes in ICU." |

## Process

1. Write the **raw version first** (truthful, direct)
2. Then adapt into **2-3 variant tones** — pick different emotional registers
3. Deliver all versions together with a short note on what each emphasizes
4. Let Beer pick or mash up elements from different versions

## Delivery format

```
Here's a few ways to tell it:

**1. Raw** — [short version]
**2. Poetic** — [short version]  
**3. Riddle** — [short version]

Which feels right?
```

Beer typically picks one, sometimes says "mash them" or asks for adjustments.
