# 2026 Platform Algorithm Strategy Guide

> Research-backed insights from automated cron learning. Use this guide when crafting posts.

## Instagram — Ranking Signals (Priority Order)

| Signal | Weight | What to optimize for |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| **DM sends** | #1 strongest | Create content people want to share with friends |
| **Saves** | #2 | Carousels with recap on final slide (triggers "second chance" distribution) |
| **Same-day freshness** | +50% reach | Post daily if possible |
| **Keyword captions** | High | Instagram indexes captions for search — write natural language, not hashtag spam |
| **Reels < 90s** | High | Seamless loop + 1-2s hook |

**Content framework:** Teach (carousels/how-tos) → Show (results/case studies) → Connect (behind-the-scenes)

**Hashtags:** Secondary ranking signal now. Caption keywords matter more.

## LinkedIn — Ranking Signals (Priority Order)

| Signal | Weight | What to optimize for |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| **Dwell time** | #1 | Long-form posts that keep readers reading |
| **Saves** | 5x reach vs likes | Saveable frameworks, checklists, templates |
| **Personal profile** | 8x vs company pages | Always post from Beer's profile, not the page |
| **Golden hour** | Critical | First 60 min determines reach — reply to EVERY comment |
| **Semantic reading** | High | Algorithm reads for meaning, not keywords — write naturally |
| **Engagement bait** | ❌ Penalised | Avoid "Comment YES" — actively demoted |

**Best posting time:** Tue-Thu 15:00-16:00 UTC

**Link placement:** Put external links in the first comment, not the post body — avoids reach penalty.

## Content Formula for Beer's Story

1. **Lead with a specific moment** (Cork shelter, April 15, the decision point) — not the mission statement
2. **4-part structure:** Problem You Lived → Failed Alternatives → Decision Point → The Insight
3. **Track the right metrics:** Sends/Reach (IG), Dwell Time + Saves (LI) — not vanity likes
4. **Stick to 2–3 consistent topics** so the algorithm learns domain expertise — posts stay discoverable for weeks, not hours

## The "Teach, Show, Connect" Framework

| Phase | What | Format | Platform |
|-------|------|--------|----------|
| **Teach** | How-to, framework, "X steps to Y" | Carousel (6-10 slides) | IG + LI |
| **Show** | Results, case study, proof | Single image + story | IG + LI |
| **Connect** | BTS, personal, vulnerable moment | Text post or Reel | IG Story + LI |
