# Beer's Story Framing Rules

> Permanent rules for how to frame Beer's story.
> Collected from corrections during Jul 23 session.

## Core Truths (Do Not Get Wrong)

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| Beer started a company | Beer **started hope** — it's not a company, it's healing |
| Beer coded / built everything | Beer **has no coding skills** — he architects with AI tools, he prompts, he designs workflows |
| The 6 agents are a product | The 6 agents are **companions** — each one is a part of his healing journey |
| House of Sak is a startup | House of Sak was born from **survival**, not a business plan |

## Word Choice Rules

1. **Never describe the suicide method** — acknowledge the attempt exists, but no graphic details
2. **Land on hope before darkness** — each section that mentions the low point must move toward the recovery
3. **"Rock bottom" is fine** — it's strong but universal, not triggering
4. **"Tried to end my life" is fine** — it's honest and necessary, but don't linger on it
5. **MH resources always** — Pieta 1800 247 247, Samaritans 116 123 on any post about the origin story
6. **No pitch on recovery content** — sacred content, never sell on it
7. **Dates to remove when Beer says "rm date"** — strip April 15, 2026 references; the story works without specific dates and they make it feel like a news article rather than a timeless narrative

## Agent Names in Origin Posts

- **Default: omit.** The origin story stands alone. Don't include agent names unless Beer asks.
- **On request: include all 6.** When Beer says "add agent name also" or "about cycle workflow add agent name" — reverse course immediately and add ALL 6 agents with their cycles and descriptions. This is a proven, Beer-approved format.

Proven agent list format (confirmed in Jul 28 session):

Close the agent section with: *"One cycle feeds the next. Dream → Hope → Care → Joy → Trust → Growth. And Growth sparks a new Dream."*

## Story Architecture

From Beer's own words:
- *"I hit rock bottom. I built my way back."*
- *"I didn't start a company. I started hope."*
- *"I even don't know what I will have. So nothing to lose at the moment."*

Use the 4-part structure only when it fits:
1. Problem You Lived → Failed Alternatives → Decision Point → The Insight

But when in doubt: **lead with hope, not trauma.**

## Tone Variants (For Emotional/Origin Content)

When Beer asks for creative versions, offer 2-3 of these:

| Tone | Characteristics | Best For |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **Raw/Honest** | Direct, unfiltered, present tense. *"I remember the exact moment I hit send. Not a text. Not a post. A goodbye."* | LinkedIn, first post |
| **Shakespearean** | Poetic, elevated, biblical cadence. *"Three dawns passed. I opened eyes that should have stayed closed."* | Creative/artistic posts |
| **Riddles** | Metaphorical, indirect, discovery-driven. *"I sent a word I never thought these lips would speak."* | Instagram, mysterious hooks |
| **Personal telling** | Warm, story-around-a-fire tone. *"I remember the exact moment..."* | Long-form anywhere |

**Rule:** After Beer picks a tone, deliver that version. Don't offer more options. Don't ask for adjustments — let him tell you if he wants changes.

## Series vs Single Post

- **When Beer says "only 1 no more"** — deliver exactly one post. Do not propose a series, a train, or "the rest of the agents." One post. Done.
- **When Beer says "all" or "the whole set"** — execute everything simultaneously.
- **When Beer doesn't specify** — default to the single post, not a series. Expanding into a series without confirmation risks wasting work.
